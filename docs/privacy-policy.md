# Shagun Register privacy policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

Shagun Register is a private ledger that helps a family record gifts given and received at weddings and other functions. It is run by [DEVELOPER NAME]. If you have a question or a request about your data, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

**Want to delete your account?** See [Deleting your data](#deleting-your-data).

## The short version

- Your ledger is shared only with the people you invite to it.
- The app has no ads. Your data is not sold, or used to build advertising profiles.
- Invite-card photos and your phone's contact list are not uploaded.
- You can delete your account in the app, or by email.

## What is stored on our server

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| **Your account:** name, email address, Google account ID, chosen language | To sign you in, and to show your name and email to other members of your ledgers |
| **Your ledgers:** people's names, relations, cities and phone numbers; events, with dates and venues; gifts, with amounts, payment modes and notes; cash counts | To keep the family's record and sync it between phones |
| **Who logged what:** your account ID and a random ID for your copy of the app, kept with the gifts you log | To keep the record accurate across phones |
| **Earlier versions of edited gifts** | So a mistaken edit can be traced |
| **Sign-in tokens:** stored only in hashed form, and valid for up to 30 days | To keep you signed in |
| **Invite codes:** valid for 48 hours | To let someone join a ledger |
| **Request logs:** the address called, the result and the time taken, never the names or amounts sent | To run the service and fix problems |

**Your ledger holds details about other people,** such as relatives' names and phone numbers. Please add only what your family is comfortable keeping. If someone wants their details removed, the ledger's owner or an editor can delete them from the People tab.

## What stays on your phone

- **The ledger:** a copy is kept on your phone so the app works offline.
- **Invite-card photos:** text is read on your phone by Google's ML Kit. The photo stays in the app's temporary storage and is not uploaded by the app.
- **Your contacts:** read only after you allow it and tap *Search phone contacts*, and held in memory while you search. A contact is saved only if you pick them, and then their name and phone number become part of the ledger.
- **App lock:** your fingerprint, face or PIN is checked by your phone. The app never sees them.
- **PDF and Excel exports:** made on your phone, and sent only where you choose to share them.

If device backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own Google backup. Sign-in tokens are left out of it.

## Who else can see your data

- **Members of your ledger** can see that ledger, and the name and email of its other members.
- **Service providers** run the app for us and process data only to do that: [Render](https://render.com) hosts the server, [MongoDB Atlas](https://www.mongodb.com/atlas) hosts the database, and Google verifies your sign-in.
- **Google's ML Kit,** which reads invite cards, sends Google diagnostic and usage information: device model and OS version, app information, performance metrics, error codes, the size and format of the image, and an identifier for the app installation. Google describes this in its [ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

We share data beyond this only if the law requires it.

## Deleting your data

### Delete your account

**In the app:** open **More → Delete account**, or **Delete account** on the setup screen if you have no ledger.

**Without the app:** email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. You don't need to reinstall the app. We will complete the request within 30 days.

**Deleted straight away from our server:**
- your name, email address and Google account ID
- your sign-in tokens
- your membership of every ledger
- any ledger you own that nobody else uses, with everything in it

**What stays:**
- **Ledgers you own that others use:** these belong to a family, so you choose first. In the app you must hand each one to another member or delete it. If you email us instead, we will ask you which you want.
- **Gifts and people you added to other families' ledgers:** these stay as part of that family's record. Your account ID remains on the gifts you logged, but it no longer leads to your name or email.
- **Copies outside our server:** other phones you use are signed out, and their copy of the ledger stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. We can't reach files you exported and shared, or your own Google backups.

### Delete a ledger

The owner can delete a ledger from **More → Family members**. Every member loses access at once. The server keeps the data for 30 days in case it was deleted by mistake, then erases it for good.

### Delete a single gift or person

Deleted gifts and people disappear from the app at once. The server keeps a marked-deleted copy so every phone learns about the deletion, until the ledger itself is deleted.

## Security

The app talks to the server over HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage and stored only in hashed form on the server. Only members of a ledger can read it, and App lock can protect the app on your phone. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your Google account and your phone protected.

## Children

The app is meant for adults keeping a family record. It isn't directed at children.
